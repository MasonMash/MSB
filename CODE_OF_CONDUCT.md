# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Model Study Bridge a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

Participants should focus criticism on technical claims and evidence, communicate respectfully, disclose material conflicts of interest, protect private information, and distinguish observed results from hypotheses.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, deliberate misrepresentation, publication of private information, and sustained disruption of technical discussion.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior is inappropriate, threatening, harmful, or incompatible with this code.

Instances may be reported through the repository communication channels available on GitHub. Do not include secrets, personal information, or sensitive security details in a public report. Reports will be reviewed with attention to safety, privacy, and proportionality.
