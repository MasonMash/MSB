# Evidence Ledger

## 1. Purpose

This document is the publication-facing index of evidence supporting Research Draft 0.1.

Its purpose is not to reproduce the experimental transcripts. It records the smallest set of non-duplicative findings needed to understand why the present protocol requirements exist.

Each entry is assigned an evidence class.

## 2. Evidence Classes

### `OBSERVED_EXPERIMENT`

A fact measured directly by the execution harness.

### `TRACE_VERIFIED_OBSERVATION`

A fact independently recomputed from stored public traces.

### `MODEL_GENERATED_ANALYSIS`

A proposal, critique, objection, proof sketch, synthesis, or decision generated by a participating model.

### `METHODOLOGICAL_LIMITATION`

A verified property of the study design that restricts what may be concluded.

### `HARNESS_FAILURE`

A verified defect or fail-closed behavior in the experimental infrastructure.

### `DETERMINISTIC_CONFORMANCE`

A result reproduced by local deterministic tests without a model call.

### `EXCLUDED_EMPIRICAL_CLAIM`

Empirical-sounding model output that was not measured or independently verified by the harness.

## 3. Canonical Findings

### E-001 — EXP-001-R2 completed the planned heterogeneous panel

**Class:** `OBSERVED_EXPERIMENT`

EXP-001-R2 completed all 12 planned model rounds.

Observed aggregate execution metrics were:

- prompt tokens: 64318;
- completion tokens: 88646;
- total tokens: 152964;
- provider-reported total cost: USD 1.146151966; and
- sequential accumulated latency: 1540188.86 ms.

The canonical trace set subsequently passed the project's trace-integrity and forbidden-private-field storage audits.

**Interpretation:** The experiment demonstrates execution of the planned heterogeneous relay. It does not by itself establish protocol correctness or interoperability.

### E-002 — EXP-001-R2 used single-hop direct deliberation context

**Class:** `METHODOLOGICAL_LIMITATION`

Each participant received the full experiment specification and the immediately preceding public artifact.

It did not directly receive the complete accumulated public-artifact history.

**Consequence:** EXP-001-R2 evaluates sequential public-artifact propagation. It must not be represented as a full-history deliberation experiment.

### E-003 — Output-schema conformance was not uniform in EXP-001-R2

**Class:** `TRACE_VERIFIED_OBSERVATION`

The Round 8 public artifact omitted the required `unresolved_issues` top-level field.

The audit found the expected field in the other 11 canonical rounds.

**Consequence:** Schema compliance in EXP-001-R2 depended substantially on prompting rather than strict rejection at the boundary.

### E-004 — Content-addressed identifiers had not been exercised in early EXP-001-R2 artifacts

**Class:** `TRACE_VERIFIED_OBSERVATION`

Across Rounds 1 through 5:

- 78 unique `artifact_id` values were observed; and
- zero matched the experimental SHA-256 digest URN form later proposed by the models.

**Consequence:** The content-addressing mechanism discussed during the deliberation had not yet been exercised by the actual artifacts.

### E-005 — The Round 8 model-generated benchmark is excluded from empirical findings

**Class:** `EXCLUDED_EMPIRICAL_CLAIM`

Round 8 emitted an evidence item labelled `empirical_benchmark`.

The harness did not execute the benchmark represented by that output.

**Consequence:** Its quantitative language is model-generated content and must not be reported as an observed experiment result.

### E-006 — The final EXP-001-R2 judge did not accept the proposal as a complete standard

**Class:** `MODEL_GENERATED_ANALYSIS`

The Round 12 public decision reported:

`PARTIAL_CONVERGENCE_NOT_ACCEPTED_AS_COMPLETE_STANDARD`

It identified three blocking design areas:

1. explicit failure and termination semantics;
2. forward-compatible version and extension behavior; and
3. immutable artifact-history semantics.

**Interpretation:** This decision is a model-generated design judgment. It is not an independent empirical measurement.

**Use in the project:** The three blocking areas were converted into explicit targeted conformance work rather than being treated as resolved by consensus.

### E-007 — EXP-002 stopped fail-closed at Round 2

**Class:** `HARNESS_FAILURE`

The canonical EXP-002 sequence was:

- Round 1: `PASS`
- Round 2: `SCHEMA_REJECTED`

No automatic scientific rerun replaced that outcome.

**Consequence:** EXP-002 is not a full structured-vs-structured quality comparison and does not establish whether complete public history improves final deliberation quality.

### E-008 — EXP-002 exposed three concrete harness defects

**Class:** `HARNESS_FAILURE`

Post-run source and behavior audits established three defects:

1. lexical empirical-language filtering conflicted with the declared provenance model for model-generated simulation and hypothesis content;
2. provider usage associated with a schema-rejected response was omitted from aggregate accounting; and
3. the sanitized rejected public artifact and safe provider metadata were not preserved for forensic review.

**Consequence:** The recorded EXP-002 cost must not be treated as complete provider cost for the canonical attempt.

These defects were corrected prospectively. The historical EXP-002 evidence was not rewritten.

### E-009 — EXP-003 passed seven deterministic conformance groups

**Class:** `DETERMINISTIC_CONFORMANCE`

EXP-003 executed locally with:

- no model execution;
- no OpenRouter call; and
- no credit usage.

Seven grouped checks passed:

1. provenance-aware public-artifact validation;
2. content addressing and canonical digest behavior;
3. append-only artifact history;
4. failure and termination semantics;
5. version and extension behavior;
6. closure and unresolved blocking-objection detection; and
7. canonical round-trace overwrite protection while retaining mutable aggregate metrics.

**Interpretation:** These results verify the tested behavior of the implemented research profile.

They do not establish universal interoperability, adversarial security, scalability, or statistical performance.

### E-010 — The present evidence supports a research draft, not a completed standard

**Class:** `METHODOLOGICAL_LIMITATION`

The current evidence base contains:

- one completed heterogeneous twelve-round relay;
- one canonical fail-closed harness-stress attempt; and
- one targeted deterministic conformance suite.

It does not yet contain independent cross-implementation interoperability testing.

**Consequence:** The appropriate publication status is Research Draft 0.1.

## 4. Deduplication Policy

A recurring objection is represented once when multiple rounds identify the same underlying protocol defect.

The ledger therefore distinguishes **number of mentions** from **number of independent findings**.

Repeated model agreement does not increase the empirical weight of a finding unless an independent observation or test supports it.

## 5. Historical Integrity

EXP-001-R2 and EXP-002 canonical evidence sets remain immutable local source records.

The public evidence manifest records two separate digest concepts:

- source-identity manifest hashes, preserving the schema used by the original evidence audit; and
- publication manifest hashes, using the public manifest schema.

Changing a manifest schema does not redefine an earlier source-identity hash.

## 6. Publication Boundary

Raw canonical traces are not included in the initial repository snapshot.

The trace sets require a separate disclosure review before publication because a public research repository has a different disclosure boundary from a controlled local experiment environment.

## 7. Study Stop Condition

No additional paid multi-model run is required for Research Draft 0.1.

Additional replication becomes methodologically necessary only if the project introduces new claims that depend on statistical performance, comparative model quality, or reproducibility across repeated stochastic executions.
